## What Is a Commercial Fit-Out?

A commercial fit-out transforms a bare shell or Category A space into a fully functional, branded workplace. In the UAE, fit-outs are classified as:

- **Shell & Core (Cat A):** Developer delivers bare concrete shell. Tenant fits out everything.
- **Category A:** Basic services installed (HVAC, raised floors, suspended ceilings, basic lighting). Tenant adds partitions, finishes, and branding.
- **Category B:** Full fit-out including partitions, flooring, joinery, IT, AV, and branding. Most common for office tenants.
- **Turnkey:** Contractor delivers a fully operational, furnished space.

## Approval Process in Dubai

### Step 1: Landlord/Developer NOC
Required before any fit-out work begins. Submit:
- Fit-out drawings (architectural + MEP)
- Contractor trade license
- Contractor insurance certificate
- Timeline and method statement

**Timeline:** 1–3 weeks (varies by developer)

### Step 2: Authority Approvals
Depending on location:

| Location | Authority | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mainland Dubai | Dubai Municipality + Civil Defence | DM for structural; DCD for fire safety |
| DIFC | DIFC Authority | Separate approval process |
| DMCC | DMCC | JLT-specific requirements |
| Trakhees | Trakhees | Dubai World/Nakheel zones |
| Abu Dhabi | Abu Dhabi Municipality | ADM approval required |

**Timeline:** 2–6 weeks depending on complexity

### Step 3: Construction
- Works proceed per approved drawings
- No deviations without written approval
- Regular inspections by authority

### Step 4: Completion Certificate
- Civil Defence inspection (fire safety)
- Municipality final inspection
- DEWA reconnection
- Landlord handover inspection

## Choosing a Fit-Out Contractor

### Must-Have Criteria
- [ ] Dubai Municipality registered contractor
- [ ] Valid trade license (DED or relevant free zone)
- [ ] Public liability insurance (minimum AED 5M)
- [ ] Experience with your specific authority (DM, DIFC, DMCC, etc.)
- [ ] In-house MEP capability or proven MEP subcontractor
- [ ] Portfolio of similar-scale projects
- [ ] References from previous clients in UAE

### Red Flags
- No permit management capability
- Subcontracts all MEP work without oversight
- No fixed-price contract
- No snagging process
- Requests >30% upfront payment
